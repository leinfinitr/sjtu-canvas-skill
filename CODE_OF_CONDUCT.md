# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We aim to make SJTU Canvas Skill a helpful, respectful, and student-friendly open-source project. Participants are expected to communicate constructively and treat others with respect.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient in issues, discussions, and pull requests.
- Provide clear reproduction steps when reporting bugs.
- Avoid sharing private course data, credentials, or personally identifiable information.
- Keep discussions focused on legitimate learning, productivity, and project improvement use cases.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private data or credentials.
- Encouraging unauthorized redistribution of course videos, subtitles, or materials.
- Using the project to bypass platform permissions or institutional policies.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and close issues or pull requests that violate this Code of Conduct. Repeated or severe violations may result in being blocked from participating in the project.
